What is the lid fold effect?

Recent MacBooks have a hinge sensor that reports exactly how far the lid is open. BendyFree reads that lid angle and, as you close the lid, gradually blurs and darkens your desktop, so the screen appears to fold away instead of just going dark.

It's a small menu-bar app for Apple Silicon MacBooks running macOS 14 or later. It starts fading in around 110° and reaches full strength near 30°. There's no account, no license key and no Screen Recording permission, and everything runs on your Mac.

If you've seen the iPhone Duo-style fold effect on a Mac and wondered whether there's a free version, this is it. Download the DMG below, drag BendyFree to Applications, and close your lid.

FAQ

Is BendyFree really free?

Yes. There's no purchase, account or license key.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

No. It reads the lid angle sensor found in recent Apple Silicon MacBooks, and macOS 14 or later is required.

Why does macOS say it can't verify the app?

BendyFree isn't notarized yet. Right-click the app and choose Open the first time you launch it.

Does it need Screen Recording permission?

No. The blur is drawn by macOS itself, so your screen is never captured.

The effect doesn't appear when I close the lid.

Check the menu bar. If it shows a live angle, the sensor works. If it shows a dash, your Mac's sensor isn't readable. You can also drag the Test Fold slider in the menu to preview the effect.

How do I start it at login or quit it?

Open the 📐 menu bar item and choose Launch at Login or Quit BendyFree.
